# Brand Voice

Reference for drafting new copy. Compliance rules and design tokens live in `CLAUDE.md` and `public/css/variables.css`.

## Brand Personality

Next Wave Cancer Rehab should feel like a calm, experienced clinician sitting beside a patient and helping them understand the next step.

- calm, grounded, steady, mature
- clinically trustworthy and professional
- warm, compassionate, human
- hopeful without being overly cheerful
- premium without feeling corporate
- local without feeling parochial

## Brand Meaning

The name **Next Wave** reflects several layers:

1. Cancer recovery is rarely linear. It moves in waves: some large and overwhelming, others small and steady.
2. The Sunshine Coast location gives the brand a natural coastal influence.
3. The ocean represents rhythm, resilience, change, and renewal.
4. "Waves" extends beyond geography to recovery, adaptation, and healing.
5. The name is personally meaningful to Megan, a tribute to her Mum and the memory of waiting for the next wave together.

Reference waves subtly through rhythm, flow, spacing, curves, and gentle movement. The site should not look like a beach resort or wellness retreat.

## Voice

Calm, clear, warm, clinically credible, respectful, emotionally safe, reassuring, plain-English, and supportive without being patronising.

Write for people who may be anxious, fatigued, overwhelmed, or unsure what is safe.

### Use language like

- Move forward with confidence
- Safe, individualised support
- Clear guidance
- At your pace
- The right guidance, at the right time
- Rebuild strength, restore movement, regain confidence
- Feel supported, informed, and in control
- You do not have to navigate this alone
- A plan that meets you where you are
- Support through treatment, recovery, survivorship, and advanced illness
- In collaboration with your medical team

### Avoid

See the avoid-list in `CLAUDE.md`. Also avoid fear-based sales language and overly cheerful wellness language.

### Clinical claim style

Use careful qualifiers and avoid absolute promises:

- may help, can support, aims to
- where appropriate
- depending on your treatment and goals
- in many cases
- in collaboration with your treating team
- individualised to your needs

## Imagery

Imagery should feel real, calm, private, supportive, professional, grounded, human, non-performative, and connected to the Sunshine Coast.

Good:

- Megan's professional portrait
- calm clinic details and quiet treatment rooms
- hands-on care details (with consent)
- subtle coastal textures, soft horizon, wave or light imagery
- gentle movement: walking, mobility, strength, confidence

Avoid:

- generic cancer stock imagery or staged, overly happy patient photos
- cold hospital imagery or equipment-heavy photos
- strong pink ribbon imagery
- beach resort or wellness retreat clichés
- before-and-after or emotionally manipulative imagery

Use personal images only with Megan's explicit approval.
